TOYZAWORLD.COM — NEW HOMEPAGE
==============================

What is in this folder
----------------------
index.html     The new homepage (design, category cards with their links,
               "All Categories" pop-up, "Viewed" link colour, contact form).
catalog.json   Your catalogue: 325 links, 23 categories (from your dashboard export).
site.json      Homepage texts, contact details, SEO, and which categories
               each of the 8 category cards shows.
img/           The 15 pictures used on the page.

How to put it live
------------------
1. Log in to your hosting control panel (File Manager) or FTP.
2. Open the folder where toyzaworld.com's files are (often "public_html").
3. BACKUP FIRST: download your current index.html and keep it safe
   (or rename it on the server to index-old.html).
4. Upload index.html, catalog.json, site.json and the img folder
   into that same folder. Replace index.html when asked.
5. Do NOT delete dashboard.html — the footer "Dashboard" link still opens it.
6. Open https://www.toyzaworld.com in your browser and press Ctrl+F5.

Updating the catalogue later
----------------------------
1. Open your dashboard, change the Catalogue Tree as usual.
2. Click "Export current tree" and save the file.
3. Rename it to catalog.json and upload it, replacing the old catalog.json.
Visitors see the change on their next page load.

Changing texts or the category cards
------------------------------------
Edit site.json with any text editor (keep the quotes and commas), then upload it.
Each card has: "label" (the name shown), "img" (picture), "cats" (the exact
category names from your catalogue that the card shows).

Note: the page must be opened from the web server (https://...), not by
double-clicking the file on your computer, or the catalogue will not load.
